Molecular cling technology

The soft, pliable specialised silicone is engineered to cling to smooth surfaces like glass or polycarbonate through molecular attraction — the same principle used in high-end optical coatings. No adhesive required.

The water bridge principle

A single drop of water acts as a bridge, allowing the lens to slide into perfect position. As you press out the water and it dries, a vacuum-like bond forms between the silicone and your lens — firm, clear, and completely residue-free.

Fully reusable, zero residue

Because there's no glue involved, EziOptx lenses leave absolutely zero residue on removal. Peel off, rinse, reapply — on a different pair of glasses, goggles or a mask — as many times as you need.

How it works
Simple. Stick. See.

Three steps to clarity. No prescription, no optometrist visit, no fuss.

01
Choose your strength

Select the diopter that matches your correction — bifocal for reading, myopia for distance. Your current glasses prescription is a reliable guide. Not sure? Contact us and we'll help.

02
Wet and press

Clean your existing lens thoroughly. Wet one side of the EziOptx lens with water and press it onto the inside of your glasses at your desired line of sight. Press firmly to release any air bubbles.

03
See clearly, instantly

Allow to dry overnight for best adhesion. Remove and reapply whenever needed — the lenses are fully reusable. Works on virtually any frame: sunglasses, goggles, safety glasses, cycling glasses and more.
